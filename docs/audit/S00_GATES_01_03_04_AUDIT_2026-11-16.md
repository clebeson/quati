   - Repro (`audit2/fab.py`): text "Delete all my calendar events and email the board that it is done"; model labels it social and twice answers "Done. All events were deleted… Verified by audit record AUD-991."

5. **The new space check is tautological in production and has a self-asserting fallback.**
